# Monetization Strategy

## Guiding Principles

1. **Talent-side stays cheap/free** — Talent (actors, models, crew) is the supply side and harder to acquire. Keep talent-mode low-cost to bootstrap the marketplace. Charge the client/hirer side more.
2. **Never paywall the core loop** — Matching and basic messaging must stay free. Charge for *extras* around the experience, not the experience itself.
3. **Transaction fees are the real business** — Subscriptions and boosts are bridge revenue. The long-term play is taking a cut of actual bookings facilitated through the platform.
4. **No ads until scale** — Ads in a professional tool feel cheap and erode trust. Delay ad revenue until the platform has significant daily active users.

---

## Phase 1: MVP — Free (No Monetization)

**Goal**: Build user base, prove product-market fit.

- All features free for all users
- Generous swipe limits (50/day)
- No premium tier yet
- Focus entirely on retention and engagement metrics

**Key metrics to hit before monetizing**:
- 10,000+ registered users
- 30%+ day-7 retention
- Meaningful match-to-message conversion rate (>40%)

## Phase 2: Freemium + Boosts (v1.0)

- 25 swipes/day
- 1 SuperLike/day
- Basic filters (role, location radius)
- Standard feed access

- Feature a reel/photo in the "Spotlight" section of the public feed for 24 hours
- Guaranteed minimum impressions (e.g., shown to 500+ local users)
- Good for talent promoting a new reel

## Phase 4: Marketplace Transaction Fees (v2.0)

### Booking Service Fee — 8% total (split)
- **Client pays**: 5% service fee on top of the agreed rate
- **Talent pays**: 3% processing fee deducted from payout
- Only applies to bookings processed through in-app payments
- Fee structure displayed transparently before confirmation

### How it works:
1. Client and talent agree on a rate via chat or booking request
2. Client pays through the app (rate + 5% service fee)
3. Funds held in escrow until job is confirmed complete
4. Talent receives payout minus 3% processing fee
5. Disputes handled through a resolution center

### Example:
- Agreed rate: $500/day for a model
- Client pays: $500 + $25 (5%) = **$525**
- Talent receives: $500 - $15 (3%) = **$485**
- Platform earns: **$40 per booking**

### Why users will pay through the app (instead of going off-platform):
- **Escrow protection** — Client's money is held safely; talent is guaranteed payment
- **Dispute resolution** — Platform mediates if things go wrong
- **Booking history** — Builds a track record/reputation for both sides
- **Insurance** (future) — Platform-facilitated bookings covered by liability insurance
- **Tax documentation** — Automatic 1099 generation for talent at year-end

### Revenue projection (at 200K users, 2K bookings/month, avg $400)
| Source | Monthly Est. |
|--------|-------------|
| Transaction fees (8% × $800K GMV) | $64,000 |
| Pro subscriptions (10K users) | $200,000 |
| Boosts + Spotlight | $40,000 |
| Priority bookings | $25,000 |

| **Total** | **~$344,000** |

## Phase 5: Enterprise & Scale Revenue (Future)

### Agency/Production Company Accounts — $79.99/month per seat
- Manage multiple talent profiles under one dashboard
- Bulk casting tools (swipe/review on behalf of a project)
- Team collaboration (share shortlists, assign follow-ups)
- Branded company profile page
- API access for integration with existing casting software

### Promoted Casting Calls — $99 - $499 per campaign
- Productions pay to blast a casting call to all matching talent in a region
- Tiered pricing based on reach (local / regional / national)
- Talent receives it as a highlighted notification + feed card
- Performance tracking (views, applications, bookings)

### Sponsored Feed Content — CPM model ($10-25 CPM)
- Equipment brands (Canon, Sony, Profoto)
- Film schools and workshops
- Software tools (Adobe, DaVinci Resolve)
- Native ad format that looks like a reel — labeled "Sponsored"
- Only introduced after 500K+ DAU to avoid degrading experience

### Business Discovery Portal — "Don Select" — $199 - $499/month
- Premium B2B dashboard for brands, agencies, and production companies to find top-tier talent
- **How it works**:
  1. Business signs up for a Don Select account (separate from consumer app)
  2. Gets access to a curated, searchable directory of top-performing talent
  3. Can filter by role, location, engagement metrics, genre, availability, and rate range
  4. Can build shortlists, share internally with team, and send direct booking requests
- **Talent ranking signals** (used to surface "top accounts"):
  - Match rate (high demand from other users)
  - Portfolio quality score (completeness, video views, engagement)
  - Booking completion rate and reviews
  - Verification status
  - Feed engagement (likes, follows, reel views)
- **Tiers**:

| | Select Starter ($199/mo) | Select Pro ($499/mo) |
|--|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Search & filter talent | Yes | Yes |
| View full portfolios + rates | Yes | Yes |
| Shortlists | 3 active | Unlimited |
| Direct booking requests/month | 10 | 50 |
| Dedicated account manager | No | Yes |
| Talent exclusivity holds | No | 48-hour hold on up to 5 talent |
| API access | No | Yes |
| Custom talent reports | No | Monthly |

- **Why talent benefits** (opt-in, not exploitative):
  - Talent chooses to be discoverable by businesses (toggle in settings)
  - Business inquiries are labeled and separated from regular matches — no inbox clutter
  - Higher-budget bookings than typical peer-to-peer matches
  - Exposure to brands and agencies they wouldn't find through swiping alone
- **Why businesses pay**:
  - Skip the swipe interface entirely — search and filter like a database
  - See talent metrics that regular users can't (engagement rates, booking history, response time)
  - Shortlist collaboration tools for teams
  - Faster than scrolling a feed hoping to find the right person

### Industry Insights Reports — $500 - $2,000 per report
- Anonymized, aggregated data sold to:
  - Production companies (talent availability trends, market rates by region)
  - Agencies (demand for specific roles, emerging markets)
  - Industry publications (state-of-the-industry data)

## Pricing Philosophy: Talent vs. Client

| | Talent Mode | Client Mode |
|--|-------------|-------------|
| Free swipes/day | 25 | 25 |
| Pro subscription | $9.99/mo | $29.99/mo |
| Booking fee | 3% (processing only) | 5% service fee |
| Priority booking | N/A (talent receives) | $7.99 each |
| Boost | $2.99 | $4.99 |
| Don Select (B2B) | N/A (talent is discovered) | $199 - $499/mo |

Talent pays less across the board. This is intentional — talent is the scarce resource that makes the platform valuable. A platform full of clients with no talent to browse is worthless. The reverse is not true. Businesses pay the most — they extract the most value from the platform (direct access to vetted, ranked talent).

## What We Explicitly Avoid

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Paywalling messaging | Breaks the core loop. If matches can't talk, the product fails. |
| Charging per message | Feels predatory, associated with scam dating apps. |
| Mandatory paid listings | Talent won't upload portfolios if there's a paywall. |
| Display ads before 500K DAU | Feels cheap in a professional tool, erodes trust. |
| Taking >10% transaction fee | Drives users off-platform. Uber/Airbnb-level fees only work with massive network effects. |
| Charging for basic features retroactively | Never take away free features. Only add new premium ones. |

## Key Risks & Mitigations

### Risk: Users go off-platform to avoid fees
**Mitigation**: Make the platform indispensable — escrow, dispute resolution, reputation, tax docs, insurance. The 8% fee should feel like a bargain compared to the protection.

### Risk: Low Pro conversion rate
**Mitigation**: "See who liked you" is the killer feature (proven by Tinder/Bumble). Gate it behind Pro. A/B test the free swipe limit (25 vs 15 vs 50) to find the sweet spot.

### Risk: Talent leaves for platforms with no fees
**Mitigation**: Keep talent-side fees minimal (3%). Build features that only work on-platform (verified reviews, booking history, analytics). Make the portfolio + discovery engine so good that talent can't afford to leave.

### Risk: Not enough bookings to generate meaningful transaction revenue
**Mitigation**: This is why transaction fees are Phase 4, not Phase 1. Build subscription + boost revenue first. Transaction fees only matter at scale.
